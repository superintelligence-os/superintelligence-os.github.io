Privacy policy

SIOS collects no data. There are no SIOS accounts, no analytics and no SIOS servers storing anything of yours. This page explains what stays on your phone and what leaves it, to whom and when.

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Applies to SIOS 0.1 (Google edition and Pure edition) and SIOS Desktop.

What stays on your phone

Your saved conversations, phone memory, screen memory (off by default), the vault of passwords, codes and documents, the firewall log, offline dictation and translation, text in your photos (OCR) and the photo editor run on the phone itself. Sensitive data is stored encrypted and can be deleted from Settings.

Google edition

The “SIOS” edition includes Google apps and services. Their use is governed by Google’s privacy policy. The “SIOS Pure” edition does not include them.

Permissions

SIOS is part of the system and can read the screen and tap in your apps to do what you ask. The AI only acts in your apps if you allow it in Settings, and what it does is recorded in the activity log, with undo.

Your control

You can switch AI, use an on-device model without internet, turn off “Find my phone”, unpair the computer and delete any data from SIOS Settings. Because the code is open, anyone can check that this policy is true.

Contact
