Ask SIOS

Tell it like you’d tell a person.

Type or talk. The AI opens apps, taps, types and shows you what it will do before sending anything important.

Tell Marta I’ll be ten minutes late

Opens your messages, writes it and shows you before sending.

Where did I see that blue sofa on Tuesday?

Screen memory finds it, even if the photo had no text.

Make me a widget with the beach weather this weekend

It appears on your home screen and updates itself.

Log this invoice for me

Reads the PDF, records the expense and suggests the date for your calendar.

If an unknown number calls, you answer

Picks up, asks who it is and gives you a summary.

Don’t let anything bother me until six

Silences what doesn’t matter and lets urgent things through.

Your stuff stays on your phone.

SIOS has no accounts, no analytics and no servers storing anything of yours. And when something has to leave, you know where it goes.

Stays on the phone

  • Your passwords, codes and documentsEncrypted, opened with your fingerprint
  • What you’ve seen on screenIf you turn on screen memory
  • Your voice when dictating, and translationsThe models work offline
  • The text in your photosRead on the phone itself
  • What the firewall blocksThe log never leaves it

Leaves, and you know where

  • What you ask your AITo the provider you choose, or nobody with an on-device model
  • Where your phone isEncrypted over Nostr: only your computer can read it
  • Models you downloadFrom Hugging Face, when you turn on a feature
  • Weather, routes, parcelsTo public services, only when you ask

Everything it brings.

A complete system, not an app on top of Android.

Security

Vault
Passwords that fill themselves in, verification codes and documents, encrypted with your fingerprint.
Firewall
Blocks trackers and ads, and lets you cut internet access per app.
Find my phone
From your computer: location, ring, lost mode, lock or wipe, and a photo of whoever gets the PIN wrong.
Emergencies
Five presses of the power button call for help. Medical ID on the lock screen and fall detection.

Offline

Dictation and translation
Whisper or Vosk for dictation and live translation, on the phone itself.
Photo text and scanner
Reads receipts, invoices and screenshots, and scans documents with perspective correction.
An AI on the phone
A model that works without internet, chosen for your phone’s memory.
Screen memory
Off by default. Turn it on and find what you saw, images included.

Every day

Home and widgets
Ask for a widget and it appears. Stacks that pick what to show you at each moment.
Keyboard and phone
A modern keyboard with a shuffled PIN pad, and a phone app that answers and summarises for you.
Camera and photos
A pro camera and an editor with magic eraser, background removal and editing with words.
Free app store and desktop mode
F-Droid apps with no accounts, and the phone as a computer when you plug in a monitor.

With your computer

SIOS Desktop
For Mac and Windows: installs SIOS over USB and moves your photos, contacts and apps.
Continuity
Shared clipboard, your notifications on the computer and files both ways.
Safe updates
The system updates in the background and rolls back on its own if something fails.
Built to last
Based on LineageOS and Android 16, for phones running Android 13 or later.

Two editions.

The same SIOS, with or without Google.

SIOS

For people who need Google apps.

  • Play Store and Google services
  • WhatsApp, banking and Maps without workarounds
  • Built with the scripts in the repository

SIOS Pure

For people who want nothing from Google.

  • No Google services
  • Neo Store, Aurora Store, CoMaps and Thunderbird
  • The edition that will be published for download

Installing it.

With SIOS Desktop and a cable, no commands.

  1. Connect your Android

    Open SIOS Desktop and plug the phone in over USB with debugging on. It tells you if it’s compatible.

  2. Try it without erasing anything

    SIOS boots as a temporary system. Restart and you’re back on your Android, exactly as it was.

  3. Keep SIOS

    If you like it, install it for good and move your photos, contacts and apps from your old phone.

SIOS 0.1 is in development. It runs on the Android emulator; it hasn’t been tested on real phones yet and there are no official downloads yet.
